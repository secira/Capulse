This is actually one of the most important design decisions for **TargetCapital.ai** because the **holding period determines which factors deserve the highest weight**.

Looking at your current model, I can infer the intended holding period from the inputs.

| Component          | Typical Horizon |
| ------------------ | --------------- |
| RSI                | 3–10 days       |
| EMA 9/20/50        | 1–8 weeks       |
| Momentum (5d/20d)  | 1–4 weeks       |
| SuperTrend         | 1–6 weeks       |
| News / Qualitative | 1–12 weeks      |
| Trend Analysis     | 2–8 weeks       |
| Risk Analysis      | Months          |
| Market Context     | Days–Weeks      |

The engine is therefore **not** a day trading model, nor is it a long-term value investing model.

## My estimate of the current holding period

Your current I-Score is best suited for:

> **2 weeks to 3 months**

It is essentially a **swing-position investing model**.

# I don't recommend having only one I-Score

Institutional investors don't use one model.

They use different models for different investment horizons.

I'd recommend creating **three versions of the I-Score**.

---

# I-Score Swing

Holding Period

**5–20 trading days**

Use for:

* Swing traders
* Momentum investors
* Positional traders

Weighting:

| Component      | Weight |
| -------------- | -----: |
| Technical      |    35% |
| Trend          |    25% |
| Market Context |    15% |
| Risk           |    10% |
| AI News        |    15% |

Indicators:

* RSI
* EMA
* SuperTrend
* Volume
* Relative Strength
* PCR
* VIX

Ignore valuation.

---

# I-Score Investment (Recommended Default)

Holding Period

**1–6 months**

This should become the **default score** shown to users.

Weighting:

| Component              | Weight |
| ---------------------- | -----: |
| Technical              |    25% |
| Fundamental            |    20% |
| Trend                  |    15% |
| AI Market Intelligence |    15% |
| Risk                   |    10% |
| Valuation              |    10% |
| Market Context         |     5% |

This balances price action with business quality and valuation.

---

# I-Score Wealth

Holding Period

**1–5 years**

Designed for investors rather than traders.

Weighting:

Technical indicators should contribute very little because short-term price swings are much less important over multi-year horizons.

# One Engine, Three Outputs

The good news is that you **don't need three separate engines**.

You already compute most of the required data.

The pipeline can remain the same:

```text
Qualitative
Quantitative
Trend
Risk
Market
Fundamental
Valuation
Institutional
```

The only difference is the **aggregation layer**.

For example:

```text
I-Score Swing

Technical      35%
Trend          25%
Risk           10%
Market         15%
AI             15%
```

```text
I-Score Investment

Technical      25%
Fundamental    20%
Trend          15%
Risk           10%
Valuation      10%
Market         5%
AI             15%
```

```text
I-Score Wealth

Fundamental    35%
Valuation      20%
Institutional  15%
Risk           15%
AI             10%
Technical       5%
```

The computation cost barely changes because you're simply applying different weights to the same underlying component scores.

# My Recommendation for TargetCapital.ai

Since your platform already includes:

* F&O Signals (intraday)
* Portfolio Analysis
* AI Research
* Wealth creation

I would **brand the scores by investment horizon** rather than exposing technical names.

| Product Name          | Holding Period    | Target User           |
| --------------------- | ----------------- | --------------------- |
| **Opportunity Score** | 5–20 trading days | Swing traders         |
| **Investment Score**  | 1–6 months        | Most retail investors |
| **Wealth Score**      | 1–5 years         | Long-term investors   |

The platform can still calculate a single underlying set of component scores, but present the three horizon-specific scores side by side. This gives users a much richer view. For example:

| Score             |                                                  Value |
| ----------------- | -----------------------------------------------------: |
| Opportunity Score |                 **92** (excellent short-term momentum) |
| Investment Score  |                  **74** (good medium-term opportunity) |
| Wealth Score      | **58** (business is expensive for long-term investors) |

That immediately tells the user that a stock may be an excellent swing trade but not necessarily a compelling long-term investment—or vice versa. I think this would be a significant differentiator for TargetCapital.ai because very few retail platforms explicitly align their scoring model with the investor's intended holding period.
