I would **not ask Replit to redesign the engine in one shot**. The architecture is already very good. Instead, implement improvements in phases so you can measure their impact on score distribution, recommendation quality, and backtesting accuracy.

Below are the exact change requests I would feed into Replit.

---

# Phase 1 – Remove Score Correlation (Highest Priority)

## Change 1: Reduce Correlation in Quantitative Score

### Current Problem

RSI, EMA and Momentum all reward the same price movement, causing the same bullish move to increase the score three different times.

### Required Change

Replace the Quantitative score with:

```text
Relative Strength vs Nifty      25%
EMA Structure                   20%
Momentum                        20%
RSI                             15%
SuperTrend                      10%
ATR Efficiency                  10%
```

### New Requirements

Add Relative Strength calculation:

```
20-day Relative Strength vs Nifty
60-day Relative Strength vs Nifty
120-day Relative Strength vs Nifty
```

Stocks outperforming the Nifty should receive higher scores.

ATR Efficiency:

```
Trend Move / ATR

Higher efficiency
Higher score
```

Do not allow RSI, EMA and Momentum to dominate the Quantitative score.

# Phase 15 – Rebalance the Overall I-Score

## Final Recommended Architecture

Replace the current weighting with the following:

| Component                | Weight |
| ------------------------ | -----: |
| Technical Analysis       |    25% |
| Fundamental Analysis     |    20% |
| Trend Analysis           |    15% |
| Market Intelligence (AI) |    15% |
| Risk Analysis            |    10% |
| Market Context           |     5% |
| Valuation                |     5% |
| Institutional Quality    |     5% |

This creates a more balanced score that evaluates **how the stock is trading (Technical & Trend), how the business is performing (Fundamental), what the market is saying (Market Intelligence), what the risks are (Risk), whether the broader environment is supportive (Market Context), whether the price is reasonable (Valuation), and whether sophisticated investors are accumulating or exiting (Institutional Quality).**

## Implementation Priority for Replit

I recommend implementing the changes in this order:

1. **Reduce correlation in Quantitative scoring** (highest ROI, low effort).
2. **Add Fundamental Analysis node**.
3. **Merge Qualitative and Search into a Market Intelligence node**.
4. **Expand Trend Analysis with Relative Strength and market structure**.
5. **Improve Risk Analysis and split Market Risk vs Fundamental Risk**.
6. **Enhance explainability with component-level breakdowns and AI-generated investment thesis**.
7. **Add Valuation and Institutional Quality modules**.
8. **Rebalance the overall I-Score weights after sufficient backtesting**.

This sequence minimizes disruption while progressively transforming the I-Score from a predominantly technical ranking system into a comprehensive investment intelligence engine suitable for long-term investors as well as swing traders.
